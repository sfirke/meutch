About Meutch

Help a neighbor, help the world.

Our mission

1-click ordering from Amazon is convenient, but bad for the planet and our souls. Meutch levels the playing field, making it easy to borrow the item from a neighbor.

Humans thriving has always come from working together. Meutch unites people to address problems big and small.

On a practical scale

  • We often need something just for a weekend. A ladder, a camping tent, a booster seat for when our niece visits. It doesn't make sense to buy these things if you'll never use them again.
  • Many of us have items that yearn to be used more. A canning pot, a sewing kit, a bike repair stand: these useful things sit idle 99% of the time.

And writ large

  • Consumerism empties our wallets and trashes the planet. We can't go on like this.
  • We're surrounded by objects yet lack relationships with our neighbors.

Let's build community together

Together we can build community trust, save money, and slow the engine of consumerism. Let's stop shopping and start sharing.

Open source and community driven

Meutch is open-source software. All of our code is available on GitHub, and we welcome bug reports, feature requests, and contributions from the community.

Questions

It's short for "mutual aid". That's why it's pronounced "mewch," like the start of "mutual." Peter Kropotkin popularized the term in his essay arguing that humans (and other animals) helping each other has been key to their evolutionary success.

Meutch does not make money and will never be commercialized. Users aren't surveilled, data isn't sold, there are no ads.

Hosting costs are currently paid by Sam out of pocket. If this thing takes off, we'll set up a crowdfunding model.

We have set up trust structures to mitigate this, like using real names and the ability to share only with people you are connected to.

But this attitude is part of the problem we are trying to work through. Despite what TV tells us, humans are generally good and cooperative. The fear of the worst has driven us apart.

Buy Nothing and Freecycle groups were a good start! Meutch aims to improve on those in a few ways:

  • Structured and searchable. Instead of scrolling a feed of random objects, you can search for what you need.
  • More privacy. You can choose to share with people you know, or people they know.
  • Open to anyone, including those who are Facebook-free.
